# Revision results on realistic populations (2026-11-07)

Numbers produced for the ApJS revision (AAS82367) by the scripts in this directory on the
analysis server (`/root/autodl-tmp/apjs_rev/`). Every table below is a post hoc read of files
under `work_<CFG>/results/`; nothing here changes the frozen protocol of
`experiments/reproducibility/`. Interval brackets are 95% block-bootstrap intervals
(10,000 replicates for the core table, 2,000 for the extras) unless stated otherwise.

## 1. Populations

Drawn with `ler` 1.0.2 (`ler_population_rev.py`, seed 20261107): GWTC-3 power-law + peak
masses, star-formation-rate merger history with delay, z <= 10, EPL + external-shear galaxy
lenses with an SDSS-type velocity-dispersion function, sources uniform inside the caustic,
one-year window. Detection = two images with network optimal S/N >= 8 (`ler` pdet with
`gwsnr` `interpolation_no_spins`, IMRPhenomD). Strain generated by `generate_strain_rev.py`
with the submitted catalog layout (24 s at 4096 Hz, whitened, peak at N-1500, Hann fade,
IMRPhenomXPHM, f_min 20 Hz); the stored channel is the loudest detector of the network.
Systems with detector-frame total mass above 800 Msun are excluded (XPHM has no power above
20 Hz for them; 105 lensed and 57 unlensed systems at O4). Pairs with delay below 60 s are
excluded (overlapping signals; 490 at O4).

## 5. Point-mass lens-profile robustness (ET)

`generate_pm_robustness_rev.py`: the same 0228 BBH sources lensed by point masses, M_L
log-uniform in 1e8-1e10 Msun, p(y) proportional to y up to y = 1, accepted when both images
reach network S/N 8 and the delay exceeds 60 s. ET: 2339 of 2500 sources accepted (1637
evaluation positives). Scored by the galaxy-trained PI-ResNet, threshold recomputed on the
same background:

| P_f | point-mass efficiency | galaxy efficiency (Section 2) |
|---|---|---|
| 1e-2 | 8.0% [6.9, 9.5] | 13.2% |
| 1e-3 | 2.4% [1.7, 3.4] | 5.3% |
| 1e-4 | 0.8% [0.6, 1.0] | 1.3% |

At the galaxy thresholds the point-mass set gives 8.7% / 2.5% / 0.5% with achieved P_f
1.2e-2 / 9.6e-4 / 9e-5, so the thresholds transfer. This supports the
recommendation to keep point masses only as a robustness test.

## 7. Cost comparison with the published posterior-overlap numbers

Read from Barsode, Goyal & Ajith, arXiv:2412.01278 (PO2.0): 65% efficiency at a pairwise
false-alarm probability of about 2e-6 for galaxy-lensed BBHs at O4 HLV sensitivity (SIE
lenses, power-law + peak masses, detection = network S/N 8 with two detectors above 4, two
images detected). The paper quotes no measured per-pair run time: Sec. I speaks of "the
promise of ... evaluation times of a few minutes", Appendix C of a veto that removes about
40% of unlensed pairs within a second, and Appendix D.4 of about half a CPU-hour of parameter
estimation per injection. An efficiency of "54% at 1e-6 in under a second" is not in that
paper, and arXiv:2605.10333 (Barsode, "BB plot") is a Bayes-factor diagnostic with no
lensed-pair efficiency. The cost model (`experiments/apjs_revision/v3/cost_model/cost_model.py`,
inputs and sources in `cost_model_inputs.json`) now uses these values plus a per-event PE
term amortised over the pairs each event enters. With the realistic ET efficiencies:

- PI-ResNet alone at P_f = 1e-3 recovers 5.3% of lensed pairs and sends 1e-3 of all pairs to
  joint PE, so its compute per recovered lensed pair (1.6e3 CPU-s at one CPU-day of PE) is
  far above the overlap statistic's (30-490 CPU-s depending on the assumed 1-300 s per pair).
  "PI-ResNet instead of the overlap statistic" is therefore not supported.
- As a pre-filter in front of the overlap statistic the chain costs 10x (P_f = 1e-1) to
  1000x (1e-3) less compute per candidate pair than the overlap statistic alone, but at a
  combined efficiency of 0.23 (1e-1) to 0.035 (1e-3) against 0.65. The compute saving is
  bought with efficiency; on a 200-event catalogue the overlap-only search costs 0.05 CPU-yr,
  which is affordable, so the pre-filter has no case at O4 scale. The case, if any, is at ET
  scale (5e9 pairs per year; 9.5e3 CPU-yr for the overlap-only search, 10 CPU-yr with the
  pre-filter at 1e-3), and there the efficiency loss must be stated.

With the O4 (warm) efficiencies the picture is the same: PI-ResNet alone at 1e-3 recovers
1.9% and costs 4.5e3 CPU-s per recovered lensed pair against 30-490 for the overlap statistic;
the pre-filter chain saves the same factors of compute at combined efficiency 0.19 (1e-1) and
0.013 (1e-3). Tables: `cost_model_table_{ET,O4}.csv`, `cost_model_catalogue_scale_{ET,O4}.csv`,
figures `fig_cost_model_{ET,O4}.pdf`.

## 9. Later finding (2026-11-08), superseded by v3

`pair_rows` in `generate_strain_rev.py` placed image 1 at the `ler` source time rather than at its own
`ler` arrival time (source time plus its time delay), so the antenna pattern of each lensed injection
was not the one `ler` evaluated detectability with; the relative delay is exact. The v2 catalogs here
carry this. It widens the injected S/N distribution about the selected one (in the v3 ET build, second
images below network S/N 8 from 20 Hz: 9.8 % against 7.6 % in ler's own selection; O4: 34 % against 21 %),
so the v2 efficiencies, O4 above all, are biased low. v3 (`v3/RESULTS_V3.md`) places images at their
arrival times; see Section 9 there.
